FAQ

Does Rare Reserve own the bottles?

No. The concept is a marketplace connecting customers with licensed partner shops. The platform does not maintain virtual inventory.

Will the website collect PAN details?

This demo does not collect, upload, store, or validate PAN details. A production implementation would require a compliant age/identity verification provider and appropriate privacy safeguards.

Can I place an order on this demo?

No. Product cards and tracking are illustrative only. No checkout, payment, or alcohol purchase is enabled.
